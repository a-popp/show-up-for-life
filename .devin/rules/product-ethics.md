When working on notifications, availability, matching, reliability, visibility, moderation, or social pressure mechanics, check `design.md` first. Protect user wellbeing and agency: no shaming, surveillance, coercive streaks, manipulative urgency, public failure signals, or sensitive inferences. Prefer private, configurable, reversible, consent-forward behavior.
