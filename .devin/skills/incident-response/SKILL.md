---
name: incident-response
description: Guide credential-leak, abuse, vulnerability, or service-incident triage
---

Guide lightweight incident triage. Contain first, preserve evidence, avoid destructive actions, and create a timeline. For credential exposure, rotate/revoke before any git-history cleanup; deletion alone is not remediation. For abuse or vulnerability reports, protect potentially affected users and avoid public disclosure of sensitive details. Produce immediate actions, evidence to collect, mitigation options, follow-ups, and communication notes. Ask before any mutation.
