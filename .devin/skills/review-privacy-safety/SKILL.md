Perform a read-only privacy and trust/safety review of the requested files, diff, or design. Check data minimization, visibility/eligibility, sensitive inference, consent, export/delete, anti-surveillance, stalking/harassment/coercion risk, and anti-shaming requirements. Report blockers separately from recommendations with citations.
